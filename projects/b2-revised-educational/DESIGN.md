# B2 educational replacement

## Intent
The user reports TikTok violation on B2 and asks for inspection and correction. Exact notice and whether this was organic, branded or paid placement are pending. Original B2 promotes financing with age/ID/no-income-verification claims, amounts and an application CTA. Remove these claims and solicitation in a substantive alternative, not by hiding words. No assertion that TikTok will approve.

The replacement is a text-led financial-literacy short. It does not preserve the original speaker's speech or silently attribute new advice to their face. No new claims about the original provider, rates, fees, licences or eligibility are invented. Retain readable outlined typography, clean icons, restrained motion, original music, no photographic zooms.

## Colors
- #102A2D: full canvas deep teal, not a border around video.
- #FFFFFF: principal type.
- #85E9DE: rules, step numbers and outline illustrations.
- #D2E2DF: body text.
- #17383A: restrained diagram fills.

## Typography
Local Be Vietnam Pro ExtraBold (OFL); main headings 66–84px, body 34–38px, labels 24px. Wide 70px margins. Content in centre, inside a 720×1280 native portrait canvas.

## Motion
No footage zoom. Each new scene crossfades over the visible previous scene for 0.35s, then small text/graphic entrances with power3, sine and expo easing. Static holds allow reading. No rapid glitch or flashing.

## Avoid
No lender promotion, age/amount promises, application/contact CTA, fake licence details, invented rates or guarantee of policy approval. No original talking-head video with substituted speech. No decorative text boxes or unrelated footage.
