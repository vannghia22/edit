# Visual direction — /editvideomau

Use the reference video's bold white/yellow outlined typography, adapted to this warm indoor talking-head clip. Show the continuous portrait edge to edge. Use only a subtle corner vignette (maximum 18% black at outer corners), with no opaque top/bottom mattes. Retain burned-in source captions/headlines rather than duplicating or blurring them. Preserve the face in the clear central area. Add concise line illustrations for identification, documents, and messaging only when mentioned.

## Colors
- #101116 — dark matte
- #FFFFFF — main text and line illustrations
- #FFE66B — keywords and numerical emphasis
- #C5C7CC — secondary details

## Typography
Be Vietnam Pro ExtraBold, embedded locally, supports Vietnamese. Bold uppercase conversational captions, at most two readable lines. No invented product branding.

## Motion
0.15–0.3 second slide/pop entrances. Gentle 2–4% reframing at topic changes. Continuous source footage; do not add unrelated scene transitions. Timed typography and illustration changes carry the pacing. CTA arrow and chat illustration only near the end.

## Avoid
- Large bordered cards, persistent frames, unrelated stock footage.
- Covering the face, layering new captions over visible old captions.
- Inventing eligibility, approval, rates, contact details, or guarantees.
- Copying reference account watermarks.
